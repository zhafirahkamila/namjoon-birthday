To Namjoon,

Happy birthday.

Thank you for the books you carried, and the ones you left open for us.

Thank you for museums we would not have entered if not for your quiet enthusiasm — for the plants you named on livestreams, for the sea photographs, for the sentences that arrived exactly when we needed them.

Thank you for choosing to keep going. And for saying, so many times, that ordinary life is enough.

We wish you a soft day. A long walk. A book you love and a page you dog-ear. A quiet meal. A room full of the people who understand you.

And if it is not too much to ask — one more song, when you are ready.

— ARMY, always.
